Learn how money, markets and the financial system work, one year at a time.

Every turn is one year. You split your money across investments, then the economy moves on: interest rates, inflation, booms, recessions, crashes and surprises. Watch what happens, read why, and find a strategy you could actually stick with.

  • 📈 Compound interest & time in the market
  • 🔥 Inflation, and real vs. nominal returns
  • 🏦 Central banks, commercial banks and how money is created
  • 📉 Why bond prices fall when rates rise
  • 🧺 Diversification, risk and return
  • 💸 Fees, trading costs & Abgeltungsteuer
  • 🧠 Panic selling and other behaviour traps
  • 🛟 Emergency funds, liquidity & deposit insurance
